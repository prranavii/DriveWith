# DriveWith Vehicle Policy & Computer Vision Inspection

## Vehicle Eligibility
- Customer vehicles must have valid RC (Registration Certificate), active Third-Party Insurance, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate.
- Transmission types supported: Manual and Automatic (CVT, DCT, AMT, Torque Converter).
- Vehicle categories: Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Luxury.

## Computer Vision Vehicle Inspection
- Before trip start: Customer uploads 6 key images (Front, Rear, Left, Right, Dashboard, Odometer).
- After trip completion: Driver uploads matching 6 post-trip images.
- The DriveWith OpenCV Visual Inspection Engine analyzes pre- and post-trip images for visual discrepancies (scratches, dents, lamp damage).
- The system generates a objective Vehicle Inspection Report noting potential visual differences.
