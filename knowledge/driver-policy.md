# DriveWith Driver Verification & Quality Standards

## Driver Verification
- All DriveWith drivers undergo 7-step background verification:
  1. Valid Commercial Driving License (minimum 3 years driving experience).
  2. Police Verification Certificate & Aadhaar authentication.
  3. Skill Passport evaluation (manual/automatic transmission certification, SUV/Luxury vehicle handling, highway & night driving experience).
  4. Pre-onboarding vehicle safety & defensive driving assessment.

## Code of Conduct
- Drivers must maintain professional demeanor, clean attire, and adhere to zero-tolerance alcohol/substance policies.
- Drivers are forbidden from altering vehicle settings or operating vehicles outside customer instructions.
