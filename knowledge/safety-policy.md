# DriveWith Safety & Real-Time Monitoring Policy

## Autonomous Safety Agent Monitoring
- Every trip is continuously monitored by the DriveWith Safety Agent using real-time GPS telemetry.
- Telemetry triggers include:
  - Unexpected route deviation exceeding 500 meters from optimal path.
  - Unscheduled stop exceeding 8 minutes.
  - Driver speed anomaly or loss of GPS signal.
- In the event of a safety alert, the Safety Agent initiates driver verification, sends customer status check, and alerts the 24/7 Safety Command Center.

## Emergency Button & Response
- The in-app Emergency SOS button broadcasts live vehicle coordinates to designated emergency contacts and local dispatch.
- DriveWith is a professional driver service and operates in coordination with civic emergency services.
