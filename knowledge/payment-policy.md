# DriveWith Payment & Fare Structure

## Fare Calculation
- Base Fare: ₹250 (includes first 5 km).
- Distance Rate: ₹18 per km.
- Time Rate: ₹2 per minute for trips over 60 minutes.
- Night Surcharge: 15% surcharge for trips between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM.
- Emergency Booking Surcharge: ₹150 priority dispatch fee for instant medical/family emergency bookings.

## Payment Methods
- Payments are processed securely via Razorpay, Credit/Debit cards, UPI, NetBanking, and DriveWith Wallet.
- Zero cash handling required; all payments are verified digitally upon trip completion.
