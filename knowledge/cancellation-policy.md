# DriveWith Cancellation & Rebooking Policy

## Customer Cancellations
- Free cancellation is permitted up to 15 minutes before the scheduled pickup time.
- Cancellations made within 15 minutes of scheduled pickup incur a nominal cancellation fee of ₹100.
- If the driver has already arrived at the pickup location, a wait fee of ₹50 per 10 minutes applies.

## Driver Cancellations & Autonomous Rebooking
- Drivers are subject to strict reliability SLAs (maximum 2% cancellation rate threshold).
- If a driver cancels an accepted booking, the DriveWith Autonomous Resolution Agent immediately activates.
- The Resolution Agent automatically searches nearby compatible drivers within a 5 km radius matching vehicle transmission and experience requirements.
- The replacement driver is assigned within 30 seconds, and the customer is notified via SMS and push notification without additional charge.
- If no replacement driver is available, the customer receives a ₹200 wallet credit and priority rebooking queue status.
